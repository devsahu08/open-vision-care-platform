The problem

Children are falling behind, silently.

Many children with undiagnosed vision problems never realize they can't see properly — they simply fall behind in school and life. They sit in the back of classrooms, unable to read the board, and get labelled inattentive. The truth is they just can't see.

For elderly communities in developing countries, uncorrected vision means a loss of independence — no longer able to read, work, or live fully.

A single pair of glasses can change all of that. And yet, in underfunded schools and villages, even a basic eye screening is a luxury most never receive.

Our approach

Small team. Real change.

We're a group of high school students who decided a lack of access shouldn't decide who gets to see clearly. Starting with partner schools in India, we run eye camps, donate glasses, and track every patient we help — so no one who receives care today gets lost by tomorrow.

  • Eye camp visits bring screening directly to schools and villages.
  • Glasses donation matches every prescription to a working pair of frames.
  • Patient tracking makes sure follow-up care doesn't fall through the cracks.
What we do

Our four programs.

Each program covers a different part of the journey from an undiagnosed vision problem to a working pair of glasses — and a student ready to lead the next one.

01

Eye camp visits

On-site screenings at partner schools and villages, run by volunteers.

02

Glasses donation

Every prescription is matched to a frame the person can keep and use.

03

Patient tracking

Our own software keeps every patient's exam and follow-up on record.

04

Youth leadership

High school students plan camps, raise funds, and lead chapters.

Open Vision Care Platform

The software behind our camps is free for any nonprofit to run.

We built patient registration, eye exams, campaign management, and inventory tracking to run our own camps — then open-sourced it, so other organizations doing this work don't have to build it twice.
